Shared rules

A visual language every sketch follows

These are proposals too, and worth agreeing on before the layouts. They come from how the tuner works: agents do the thinking, code does the measuring, and nothing counts until val confirms it.

Agent words look different from code numbers

"trim get_container to 6 fields" ▲5 fixed

Hypotheses, plans and lessons are violet italic. Measured numbers are plain. A reader can always tell a claim from a result.

Bright means flipped, dim means unchanged

█ fixed ▓ held ░ still failing ✕ broke

Hue says pass or fail; brightness says whether the candidate changed it. Your eye goes to what changed.

One ember thing: the champion

◆ w3-4 champion ◇ start

The current baseline is the only ember-bold element on screen. When it changes hands, that is the one moment the screen celebrates.

Cyan means in flight

◐ ⠸ ━━━━━━╸ everything else is settled

Anything cyan will change soon. A still screen with no cyan means the run is waiting or done.

Never color alone

▲ ▼ ✓ ✕ ◆ ★ ◉ ● ○ ◐

Every state has a glyph, so the screen still reads on light terminals, for colorblind people, and under NO_COLOR. Toggle Light at the top to check.

Say where a number came from

85.7 val 88.5 train

Single experiments are measured on train, on their target evals only. Champions are confirmed on val. The display keeps that difference visible.
