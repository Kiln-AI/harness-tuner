Results as cards

One card per concern with three bars: Start, Best Confirmed (one run config, measured on val) and Best Unconfirmed (the best single run config for that concern, measured on train). Cards stretch and reflow to the terminal's width. Every concern is shown. The key sits on one line in the section header: on the title line when the terminal is wide enough, on its own line when it is not.

Pick a terminal width to see the reflow, or "All widths" to compare them. Sample data on an invented agent.
