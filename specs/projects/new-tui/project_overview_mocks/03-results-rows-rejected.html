Tiers
    
      
  • Start: the config the run began from. Train until the first gate, then val.

  • Champion: the current promoted config, on val. Dim means no clear change from start.

  • Unconfirmed wins (the indented lines): a successful train verdict, not promoted, not rejected at val, not already in a promoted merge. A merge's result replaces its ingredients.

One scale
    
      
  • Pass-rate evals: share of start's failures now fixed.
    • 
      
  • Rating evals: share of the distance to the top of the scale.
    • 
      
  • Cost and latency: share cut from start. Only configs whose quality held can show here.

Data
    
      
  • Train start values: the detectability report (emitted).

  • Val start and champion values: the gate's per-eval tables (computed, not emitted today).

  • Unconfirmed membership: item_outcome, promotion and merge ingredients (derived). Pooled estimate emitted; per-eval rows computed.
